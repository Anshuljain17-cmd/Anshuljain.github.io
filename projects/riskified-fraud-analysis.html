Business question

How do chargeback patterns vary with order value, product type and customer account age, and what trade-offs arise when considering an approval threshold for e-commerce orders?

Approach

  • Loaded and explored anonymised transaction fields such as order price, classification score, product type, customer account age and outcome label.
  • Compared chargeback patterns across digital and tangible products.
  • Used distribution plots, category comparisons and trend visualisations to inspect relationships between customer/order attributes and chargebacks.
  • Explored a score threshold against a target of approving 90% of orders, as described in the project brief.

Findings recorded in the project

  • Newer customer accounts were associated with higher chargeback risk in the explored data.
  • The README reports chargeback concentration among lower-value orders, around the ₹1,500 region.
  • Digital goods showed different chargeback patterns from tangible goods.
These are descriptive findings from the sample analysis, not proof of causal relationships or production fraud-detection performance.

What this demonstrates

Python-based data preparation, exploratory analysis, visual comparison of risk segments and translating observed patterns into potential business checks.
