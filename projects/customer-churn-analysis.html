Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

Exploratory analysis of 7,043 telecom customer records to identify churn patterns across contracts, payment methods, tenure and customer segments.

Business question

Which customer and account characteristics are associated with churn, and where could a telecom business focus retention efforts?

Dataset and method

The repository describes a telecom dataset with 7,043 records covering demographics, dependents, contract type, tenure, payment method, service usage and churn status.

  • Explored churn distribution across customer and account variables.
  • Compared churn rates by contract type, payment method, tenure and senior-citizen segment.
  • Used count plots, bar charts and other visual summaries to communicate differences.

Key findings reported

  • Month-to-month customers: 42% churn, compared with 11% for one-year and 3% for two-year contracts.
  • Electronic-check customers: 45% churn, compared with approximately 15–18% for the other payment groups.
  • Customers with less than one year of tenure: 50% churn, compared with 15% for customers with more than three years.
  • The README reports 41% churn among senior citizens versus 26% among younger customers.
Percentages are reproduced from the project's README and should be rechecked against the notebook before being used in a business decision.

Business recommendations explored

  • Test incentives that encourage suitable customers to choose longer-term contracts.
  • Investigate onboarding and early-tenure experience, where churn appears higher.
  • Study payment friction and customer preferences before changing payment incentives.
  • Design segment-specific retention experiments and measure their impact.

What this demonstrates

Customer segmentation, exploratory data analysis, visual storytelling and converting descriptive patterns into testable retention hypotheses.

Next improvements
