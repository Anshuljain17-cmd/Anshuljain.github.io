Business question

How can a retail team monitor sales, product mix, outlet performance and customer ratings in one interactive report?

Data preparation and modelling

  • Connected Excel/CSV source data to Power BI.
  • Cleaned missing values, duplicates and inconsistent category values, as described in the repository.
  • Created relationships between tables and documented a star-schema modelling step.
  • Built DAX calculations for the headline KPIs.

Dashboard content

  • KPI cards for total sales, average sales, item count and average rating.
  • Donut chart comparing sales by fat content.
  • Bar chart for item-type performance.
  • Geographic/outlet breakdowns and a matrix view by outlet type.
  • Line chart showing sales by establishment age.
  • Interactive slicers and filters for exploration.

Insights described in the project

  • Sales mix varies across product attributes and outlet segments.
  • Regional preferences are relevant when comparing revenue and customer satisfaction.
  • Some item types with lower average ratings may warrant further investigation.
